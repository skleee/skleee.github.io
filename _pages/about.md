<h1 id="name">Sunkyung <strong>Lee</strong></h1>
<p class="subtitle">이선경 · 李宣璟</p>
<img class="portrait" src="{{ '/images/profile.jpg' | relative_url }}" alt="Sunkyung Lee" width="170">

I'm a Ph.D. candidate advised by Prof. Jongwuk Lee in the Data Intelligence and Learning Lab (DIAL Lab) at Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU), Korea. I received my M.S. degree in Artificial Intelligence from SKKU in 2023, under the supervision of Prof. Jongwuk Lee. Previously, I earned my B.S. in Computer Science and Engineering and B.A. in Economics from SKKU in 2021. During my Ph.D. studies, I completed research internships at **Roblox**, **Snap Research**, and **Naver**. My research focuses on **generative search and recommendation**, combining information retrieval and natural language processing. I’m interested in using large language models for **personalization and user modeling**. My CV is available [here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1SJqkRk8m8wokrBe_Qbf9Syw6DnhYNXR5/view?usp=sharing).
